## Legal & Contracts

- Legal **Contract Review Assistant**: Complete contract preliminary screening, clause extraction and risk annotation in three minutes, and output a structured review report with original text citations
- Legal Affairs **Legal Document Generation**: Enter case information to automatically generate legal documents with standard format and rigorous logic.

## Office scene

- Finance **Invoice Registration and Inspection**: Automatic entry of invoice information, authenticity inspection, and compliance testing to prevent repeated reimbursement and false invoices
- Administrative **Meeting Minutes Organization**: Upload audio or text records, automatically extract issues, resolutions, and to-do items, and generate standardized minutes
- Office **Weekly and Monthly Report Generation**: Summarize project progress and task completion status, and automatically generate a first draft of a weekly or monthly report with a clear structure
- Office **System Standards Q&A**: Build a knowledge base based on corporate system documents, and employees can check attendance, reimbursement, approval and other specifications at any time

## Education

- Education **One-click learning analysis**: Upload your transcript to automatically generate a comprehensive learning portrait, quickly locate weak links and improvement directions
- Education **Intelligent Paper Creation**: Based on the knowledge map and difficulty coefficient, automatically generate exam papers with reasonable structure and moderate difficulty.

## Operation & Creation

- Operation **Tweet Creation Assistant**: Generate the first draft of a public account article in seven seconds, supporting brand style customization and SEO optimization
- Esports **Post-game review AI assistant**: Automatically analyze game data, generate tactical review reports, mark key moments and suggestions for improvement
